Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information DimagWala Lab may collect, why it is used, the third-party services involved, and how you can request access to or deletion of your information.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

DimagWala Lab (also referred to as “we,” “our,” or “the App”) is an educational Android application developed under the DimagWala (DW) name. It provides virtual electrical engineering experiments, component libraries, circuit simulations, progress tracking and study tools.

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled when you use the DimagWala Lab Android app, sign in to it, use its learning features, view ads, or contact support.

2. Information we may collect or process

Google account and profile data

When you sign in using Google, the App may receive account information made available through Google Sign-In, including your Google account identifier, display name, email address and, where provided, profile photo. We use this information for sign-in, identifying your app account, and showing your profile.

Academic details and learning information

If you provide them, academic details such as course or study information may be stored with your profile. The App may save your experiment progress, favourites, recent practicals, observations, simulation inputs, and related learning preferences. Some of these records are kept on your device (for example, in app preferences or local web storage); account/profile data may be processed through Firebase services.

Device and usage data

Our integrated analytics, crash-reporting and advertising services may process app interactions, device or app identifiers, advertising identifiers (where available), device and operating-system information, approximate location inferred from network signals, performance measurements, diagnostic events, and crash details, depending on your device, consent settings and SDK configuration. We do not need precise GPS location to provide electrical lab experiments.

Messages to support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, device details and bug-report descriptions. Please do not send passwords, government IDs, or sensitive personal documents.

3. How information is used

  • Authenticate users with Google and manage access to the virtual laboratory.
  • Provide experiment tools, save supported learning records, and show your progress.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve App performance.
  • Measure app usage and investigate crashes or technical errors.
  • Display and measure advertisements, subject to applicable choices and laws.
  • Provide educational notifications if you grant notification permission.
  • Respond to feedback, questions, and privacy or deletion requests.

4. Service providers and data sharing

The App integrates services from Google, including:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication: account sign-in and authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: supported account/profile data operations.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: usage and engagement measurement.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: technical diagnostics and crash information.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: displaying and measuring ads; advertising data may be processed according to Google's policies and the user's consent choices.

Information processed by these services is subject to their relevant terms and privacy practices. For details, visit Google Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and How Google uses information for advertising.

We may also disclose information when legally required or to protect users, the App, and our legal rights. We do not offer users' personal information for sale directly.

5. Advertising and consent

DimagWala Lab may show banner or interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process identifiers and ad-interaction information to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and—when permitted—personalize advertisements.

Where required by law, we seek appropriate consent through the advertising consent experience. Availability of personalized ads and consent choices may vary by region. You may also manage certain advertising choices through your Android and Google account settings.

6. Device permissions and local storage

  • Internet and network state: to sign in, load online services, deliver ads and detect connectivity.
  • Notifications: to provide optional learning reminders. You can turn off notifications in Android Settings.
  • Receive boot completed: to restore scheduled learning notifications after a device restart where supported.
  • Advertising ID: may be accessed by Google Mobile Ads where permitted by Android settings and applicable law.

Progress, preferences and some observation or feedback drafts may be stored locally on your device. Removing the App or clearing its storage can delete locally saved information. Exported CSV files are saved or shared at your direction and can remain on your device after you uninstall the App.

7. Security

We use established Google/Firebase services to support authenticated access and encrypted connections for supported network communication. We also rely on operating-system security controls to protect locally stored app data. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. If you suspect an issue involving your account or records, contact us using the details below.

8. Data retention

Account and related records may be retained while your App account remains active and as needed to operate the service, respond to support requests, comply with applicable law, resolve disputes or protect service security. Firebase, analytics, diagnostic and advertising information may have service-specific retention periods. Locally stored records generally remain on your device until cleared, deleted through available App features, or removed by uninstalling the App.

When a verified account deletion request is completed, we aim to delete the associated app account and associated data under our control, except information that must be kept for legitimate legal or security reasons. Any exceptions should be explained during the deletion process.

9. Your choices and account deletion

You can manage notification permissions and some advertising settings through Android. You can clear locally stored data through Android Settings. You may contact us to ask about your account data, to correct profile information, or to request deletion of your DimagWala Lab account and associated information.

To request account deletion: email dimagwala02@gmail.com with the subject DimagWala Lab – Account Deletion Request. Specify the email address associated with your account. We may request appropriate verification before acting on the request. Do not send your password.

We will review the request, explain any required verification and any lawful retention exceptions, and communicate the outcome. Merely sending a request does not automatically delete your account. Removing the App from your phone also does not necessarily delete Firebase account data.

Request account deletion by email

Developer notice: This web page is a request channel, not an automated deletion system. Before publishing, ensure that requests are actually handled and that the App offers a discoverable in-app deletion path as required by Google Play.

10. Children and students

DimagWala Lab is intended as an electrical engineering learning tool. It is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to applicable law, contact us and we will review the matter. We do not knowingly seek sensitive personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when App features, providers, privacy practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current published version. Material changes will be communicated where legally required.
